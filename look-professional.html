About

What is Stylish Barber Bishopdale?

Stylish Barber Bishopdale is a barber and hair salon based at 11B Bishopdale Ct, in the Christchurch suburb of Bishopdale.

If you live, work or shop around Bishopdale, it's a handy local spot for your next cut, close to home and without a trip into the central city.

We've kept this page simple: where to find us, how to get in touch and how to book. If you want to know which cuts and styles we do, current prices or how long an appointment takes, the quickest way is to call us on 022 488 9873.

How do I book a haircut?

You book with a phone call. Here's how to do it.

  1. Call the shop
